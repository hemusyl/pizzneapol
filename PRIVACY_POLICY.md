# Privacy Policy for PIZZNEAPOL

**Effective Date:** September 23, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 23, 2026

Welcome to **PIZZNEAPOL** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled safely and responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our pizza ordering and delivery services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information You Provide
When registering an account or placing an order, we may collect:
- **Contact Details**: Name, email address, phone number.
- **Delivery Information**: Delivery address, street name, city, apartment/suite number, and delivery notes.
- **Account Credentials**: Securely hashed passwords used for authentication.

### B. Order & Transaction Data
- **Order History**: Items ordered, quantities, customization options, timestamps, order amounts, and promotional codes applied.
- **Payment Method Selection**: Chosen payment method (e.g. Cash on Delivery, Mobile Banking, Card). We do not store complete credit card or CVV details on our servers.

### C. Technical & App Usage Data
- Device model, operating system version, and unique installation identifiers.
- App performance data and crash logs to improve app stability and user experience.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following legitimate purposes:
1. **Order Fulfillment**: Processing, baking, packaging, and dispatching your food orders to your designated location.
2. **Customer Communications**: Sending order status updates, delivery alerts, and SMS/push notifications regarding your delivery.
3. **Customer Support**: Responding to customer inquiries, resolving order issues, and processing refunds.
4. **App Improvements**: Diagnosing technical bugs, optimizing app speed, and enhancing the user interface.
5. **Security & Fraud Prevention**: Detecting and preventing fraudulent transactions or unauthorized account access.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information to third parties. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:
- **Delivery Couriers**: Sharing your name, delivery address, and phone number with our delivery personnel to ensure accurate delivery.
- **Service Providers**: Trusted third-party cloud hosting providers, SMS gateways, and payment processors acting strictly on our behalf under data protection agreements.
- **Legal Requirements**: If required by applicable laws, court orders, or governmental regulations.

---

## 4. Data Security & Encryption

All data transmitted between the PIZZNEAPOL mobile application and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard **Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS)**. We implement strict administrative and technical measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

---

## 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to:
- Access and review the personal data associated with your account.
- Update or edit your name, phone number, and delivery address from the **Settings / Profile** tab.
- Request complete deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting our support team.

To request account deletion or data removal, please email: **support@pizzneapol.com** with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"*. Your request will be fulfilled within 30 business days.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove the information.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the application and the "Last Updated" date at the top will be updated accordingly.
